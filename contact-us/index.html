Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How do I register for an event?

You can register for any upcoming event through our Events page. Click on the event you're interested in and follow the registration link, or reach out to us directly via this form.

Can I collaborate as a speaker or expert?

Absolutely! We are always looking for passionate wellness experts, speakers, and facilitators. Select "Speaker / Expert" in the form above and tell us about your expertise.

Do you offer corporate wellness programs?

Yes, we design customized wellness programs for organizations of all sizes. From one-day workshops to long-term wellness initiatives, we tailor our approach to your team's needs.

Are your events beginner-friendly?

All our events and programmes are designed to welcome participants at every level. Whether you're new to wellness or experienced, you'll find something that resonates with you.

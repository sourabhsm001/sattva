Our Why

A Vision Born From Personal Transformation

We believe that every individual carries the seed of Sattva within them — purity, balance, and inner harmony. Our mission is to create spaces where that seed can grow.

Sattva Nation was born from a simple yet profound realization: in the rush of modern life, people have forgotten how to pause. Our founders came together with a shared vision — to bring holistic wellness out of exclusive retreats and into everyday life, making mindful living accessible to all.

What started as intimate gatherings among friends has grown into a nationwide movement, touching hundreds of lives through festivals, workshops, retreats, and corporate programmes. Yet the core remains unchanged: creating authentic spaces for transformation.

Our Path

The Sattva Journey

Key milestones that shaped Sattva Nation from a small idea into a growing wellness movement.

2020

The Seed is Planted

Priya and Arjun meet at a wellness retreat in Rishikesh. Sharing a vision for accessible holistic wellness, they begin hosting small community gatherings in Delhi.

2021

First Wellness Festival

Sattva Nation hosts its first wellness festival with 50 participants, featuring yoga, nutrition workshops, and mindfulness sessions. The community response is overwhelming.

2022

Corporate Wellness Launch

Expanding beyond individual experiences, Sattva Nation launches corporate wellness programmes, partnering with leading organizations to bring mindfulness to the workplace.

2023

National Expansion

Events and retreats expand to Goa, Mumbai, Bangalore, and the Himalayas. The community grows to 500+ members with a network of 50+ wellness experts.

2024

Sattva Iconic Awards

Launch of the Sattva Iconic Wellness Awards, recognizing changemakers in the wellness industry and cementing Sattva Nation's position as India's premier wellness platform.

2025

The Future Unfolds

With 50+ events hosted and 500+ lives touched, Sattva Nation continues to grow — launching online programmes, university collaborations, and government partnerships.
